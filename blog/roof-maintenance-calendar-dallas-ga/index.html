Year-Round Roof Maintenance Calendar for Dallas GA Homeowners

By iRoofer Contractors

June 2027 · 7 min read

Georgia weather doesn't take a season off. Spring hail, summer UV, fall leaves, winter ice — each attacks your roof differently. A quarterly routine catches the small stuff before it turns into a $15,000 replacement. Here's your calendar.

Golden rule: Never walk a steep or wet roof. Binoculars from the ground + attic check = 80% of what you need. Call a pro for the rest.

🌱SPRING (March–May)

TaskWhy It MattersDIY?
Gutter clean-outRemove winter debris. Check downspout flow — clogged gutters back up under shingles.✅ Yes
Visual roof scanFrom ground with binoculars: look for missing, curled, or granule-bald shingles.✅ Yes
Attic checkDaylight through deck? Water stains on rafters? Mold smell? These mean leaks.✅ Yes
Flashing inspectionChimney, vents, skylights — sealant cracked? Gaps? Re-caulk with polyurethane.🔧 Pro
Trim overhanging branches6+ ft clearance. Branches scrape granules; falling limbs puncture decks.🔧 Pro

☀️SUMMER (June–August)

TaskWhy It MattersDIY?
Post-storm walkaroundAfter every severe thunderstorm: check yard for shingle pieces, granule piles at downspouts.✅ Yes
Ventilation verificationRidge vent + soffit vents flowing? Hot attic = baked shingles + higher AC bills.🔧 Pro
Moss/algae checkNorth-facing slopes in shade — green streaks? Zinc/copper strips or professional soft-wash.🔧 Pro
Skylight condensationIf interior glass sweats, add a bath fan or crack the skylight vent.✅ Yes

🍂FALL (September–November)

TaskWhy It MattersDIY?
Full gutter cleanCritical — leaf dams cause ice dams in January. Install guards if trees overhang.✅ Yes
Pre-winter roof inspectionPro inspection now beats emergency tarp in January. We check 30+ points.🔧 Pro
Seal pipe bootsRubber boots around vent pipes dry-rot in 5–7 years. $15 part, $200 labor if ignored.🔧 Pro
Chimney cricket checkWide chimney needs a cricket (diverter) to shed water. Missing = guaranteed leak.🔧 Pro

❄️WINTER (December–February)

TaskWhy It MattersDIY?
Ice dam watchIcicles = heat loss. Rake snow off lower 3 ft of roof after heavy falls (from ground with roof rake).✅ Yes
Interior leak scanCheck ceilings after freeze-thaw cycles. Stains = active leak. Call immediately.✅ Yes
Attic frost checkFrost on nails/sheathing = ventilation failure. Fix before spring melt.🔧 Pro
Emergency planSave our number: {brand.phone}. 24/7 tarp service if a tree limb comes through.✅ Yes

Annual Pro Inspection: What We Check (That You Can't)

  • Shingle adhesion pull-test (wind uplift resistance)
  • Flashing integrity at every penetration
  • Attic ventilation balance (intake vs. exhaust CFM)
  • Deck moisture meter readings
  • Fastener pattern & corrosion check
  • Warranty compliance verification

We provide a 12-page photo report with priority ratings. $199 — waived if you hire us for any repair.

Quick-Reference: When to Call Immediately

  • Water dripping inside (active leak)
  • Shingles in yard after storm
  • Sagging ridge or ceiling
  • Granules washing out downspouts by the handful
  • Daylight visible through roof deck in attic
