# iRoofer Contractors — Brand Guide

> Living brand document. Source of truth for the website, ads, docs, and any
> AI-generated assets. Edit here, then regenerate downstream artifacts.

## Identity
- **Legal / public brand:** iRoofer Contractors (NEVER "Guary" in any customer-facing asset)
- **Owner:** Guary
- **HQ:** Dallas, GA
- **Service area:** Dallas GA + greater Paulding/Cobb/Douglas counties — Douglasville, Hiram, Powder Springs, Marietta, Acworth, Kennesaw, Austell
- **URL:** https://iroofercontractors.com
- **Voice:** Local, family-owned, trustworthy. Plain spoken. We show up, we fix it, we stand behind the work.

## Elevator pitch
iRoofer Contractors is a local, family-owned roofing company serving Dallas, GA and the
greater Atlanta metro. We handle roof repair, full roof replacement, and new-construction
roofing with honest pricing, clean job sites, and work we're proud to put our name on.

## Design tokens (see tokens.json)
- **Palette:** Slate (deep blue-gray structural neutral) + Rust (warm amber-orange accent).
  Rust = action, urgency, trust. Slate = stability, professionalism.
- **Fonts:** Sora (headings, sturdy/modern) + Inter (body, highly readable).
- **Radii / shadows:** soft, confident cards; no harsh edges.

## Trust signals to feature
- A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau
- 500+ 5-star reviews (5★ average)
- Local & family-owned
- Free, no-pressure roof quotes

## Logo rules
- Vector SVG only (scales to billboard or business card).
- Wordmark reads "iRoofer Contractors".
- Rust roof mark + slate wordmark. Do not recolor the mark to anything outside the rust ramp
  without explicit approval.

## Image guidance (for AI generation)
- Realistic, Southern-suburb residential roofing: ranch/Craftsman homes, asphalt
  architectural shingles, crews in safety gear. Daylight, clean skies.
- Avoid cartoonish, overly saturated, or stocky "glowing" looks.
- People should look like real local crews, not models.
